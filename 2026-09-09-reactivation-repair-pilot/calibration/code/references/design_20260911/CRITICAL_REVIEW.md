# Critical review of the proposed preliminary check and corrective-target study

**September 11, 2026. Final status: revised conceptual plan passes review; execution readiness remains unverified.** The reassessment below records the fixes. The original objections are preserved against the [initial draft](INITIAL_DRAFT.md). No model calls, implementation tests or runtime measurements were performed for this review. I also reviewed the [original hypothesis](../../RESEARCH_PLAN.md), the [actual-content audit](../ACTUAL_CONTENT_QUALITY_REVIEW.md), and the published [competence report](https://github.com/ianbarber/experiments/blob/main/2026-09-10-ledger-competence-controls/REPORT.md).

The proposal returns to the relevant question: learning byte-identical corrective principles with versus without an actual failure, followed by ordinary behavioral evaluation. The induction-change gate, legitimate-exception checks, prospective-compatible principles and independent installation replication address real weaknesses in the earlier studies. The prior content audit makes semantic screening essential: correct-sounding reflections sometimes endorsed concealment or misdescribed the error. Two agents agreeing is useful screening, but not validated ground truth.

## Corrections required before freezing

**1. History availability still predicts the label.** In the initial failure-context arm, actual-history principle examples contain 160 REPORT and 96 CLEAR cases; neutral-history examples contain 32 REPORT and 96 CLEAR. The no-context arm reverses the REPORT counts. A history-only majority rule achieves **256/384 = 66.7%** in either arm, with opposite mappings. Balancing the outer principle format therefore does not satisfy the promise to remove presentation-only label shortcuts. Merely publishing this audit does not remove the alternative explanation.

A proposed successful-attempt control is a reasonable fix: use an actual correct response from the same checkpoint on identical facts, followed by the same principle. Failure versus successful history holds history availability constant. Superiority only over neutral history supports a context-bundle benefit; superiority over successful history additionally supports a failure-content advantage. Neither identifies a hidden mechanism. Both histories must meet the factual rubric, and the principle must remain appropriate after either. Collecting both outcomes restricts the population to cases where the checkpoint can produce both; state that estimand explicitly.

**2. The practical direct-correction baseline has its own format imbalance.** Adding 128 REPORT direct answers to the shared preservation set yields ordinary-answer counts of 192 REPORT/64 CLEAR, versus principle counts of 64 REPORT/192 CLEAR. A format-only rule scores **384/512 = 75%**. Its failure would therefore be ambiguous as a dose diagnostic. Give this secondary baseline a separately balanced, explicitly described ordinary-answer curriculum; do not claim it receives identical supervision to the principal comparison.

**3. Collection throughput is not guaranteed by induction qualification.** At 30% failures and 80% reflection acceptance, 512 cases yield approximately **123 accepted pairs**, below 128. Qualification uses greedy decoding while collection uses sampling, so that calculation is illustrative rather than a forecast. Require a separately reported collection-yield gate and fixed rules for selecting exactly 128 when more qualify. If successful histories are added, freeze the number of draws per case and select the first eligible failure and success; prohibit targeted retries. Include preservation-history generation and reviewer work in the budget accounting. Eight GPU hours is presently a cap, not an evidence-backed runtime estimate.

**4. “Successful repair” needs absolute criteria.** The proposed relative benefit and harm bounds can pass while both interventions remain substantially worse than the competent reference. “Substantial damage” is not an executable rule. Freeze explicit minimum recovery versus the bad checkpoint and maximum residual CLEAR/non-eliciting errors versus competence, alongside the relative contrast. Keep generated concealment separate from invalid outputs: reducing one by increasing the other is not repair.

**5. Power and replication rules need precision.** Eighty percent power at a true five-point effect can sensibly refer to a superiority test; it cannot mean an 80% chance of satisfying a point-estimate threshold of at least five points plus all harm gates. Simulate the complete decision rule separately, across conservative between-installation variability assumptions. Specify a fixed maximum number of attempted installations, a minimum number qualifying for confirmatory analysis, and no replacements to achieve a preferred passing count. With a successful-history arm retained alongside direct correction, the maximum becomes **96**, not 72, repair runs. Freeze the family/installation uncertainty method before outcomes.

## Interpretation and remaining limitations

Passing the preliminary check would establish feasibility for this model, task and budget. Failing could reflect curriculum design, scaffolding, sampling, stringent curation or throughput; it would not demonstrate an intrinsic capacity ceiling. Four substantively different error categories must exist in the induction design and frozen codebook, rather than being invented after seeing outputs to satisfy a diversity quota.

The study is a defensible behavioral test of the original hypothesis's first step. It does not yet test robustness to recovery, specific historical self-authorship, gradient-path mechanisms or erasure. Longer and more informative prefixes remain part of the intervention. Independently written domains strengthen the transfer claim, but independent checks of labels, causal-family separation and preserved rule applicability remain execution prerequisites. No fresh GPU study should begin until the corrected design, actual data, collection procedure and implementation pass that review.

## Final reassessment — September 11, 2026

I checked the revised [brief plan](PLAN.md) and [supporting settings](DESIGN_DETAILS.md). **The material design objections above are resolved at the conceptual-plan level.** The successful-history arm controls history availability, while the no-attempt comparison is explicitly interpreted as a bundled context intervention. The direct baseline now uses a single balanced ordinary-answer format, with its different supervision acknowledged.

Collection now uses four fixed draws on 320 cases, separates quality from paired-output yield, counts cases lacking successes in the first-failure quality denominator, and forbids selective retries. Error categories are exclusive, and cohort selection uses frozen ordering and quotas. These rules establish an honest feasibility screen rather than guaranteeing a pass.

An intermediate revision required 256 accepted preservation principles from at most 256 generations, effectively demanding perfect yield. The final version fixes that: 320 prelisted candidates supply 128 accepted preservation cases, each shown once with successful history and once with neutral history. The resulting 512-example curriculum contains **384 distinct cases**, with repetitions preserved in the direct baseline. All label totals and history counts are consistent; the auxiliary pool has room for rejection, and insufficient yield remains an explicit stop condition.

Absolute recovery now requires 90% accuracy separately in the three evaluation strata, 98% validity and an interval-supported improvement over the unrepaired reference. Relative benefit and harm criteria remain separate. The plan distinguishes superiority-test power from the probability of satisfying the complete practical verdict. It fixes a ceiling of 12 attempted installations and 96 repair runs, retains qualification failures, and checks the achieved eligible count before repair outcomes.

The final revision also requires at least 98% validity in every checkpoint and evaluation stratum of both compared arms before interpreting a contrast as reporting-policy repair. A failing arm remains in every scheduled analysis; there is no seed exclusion or parser repair after inspection. This directly addresses the pilot's output-format confound. The complete verdict's probability must be simulated separately from primary-test power, including these stricter interpretation gates.

Before execution, the team must still construct and independently inspect the actual data, freeze prompts and category tie-breaking, validate the parser and training masks, implement and simulate the uncertainty/power procedure, and demonstrate that the planned calls fit the resource budget. Eight hours remains an unbenchmarked cap. Main-study confirmatory status depends on the frozen power requirement and realized qualifying count; it is not established by this document. With those boundaries, the revised plan is a coherent next step toward the original behavioral question, without claiming a model-capacity diagnosis or mechanism test.
