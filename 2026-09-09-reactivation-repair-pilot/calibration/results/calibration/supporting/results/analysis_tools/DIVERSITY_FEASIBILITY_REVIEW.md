# Diversity feasibility: interpretation review during calibration

September 12, 2026. This is an additional interpretation audit of frozen inputs, not a changed gate or a judgment of actual generated traces. The acquisition and transfer grid remains valid regardless of the later diversity outcome.

No mathematical contradiction makes the material gate impossible. The selected set has 128 records and the 20 percent cap allows at most 25 records in any normalized-text group or either reviewer's generic cluster. If every generic failure belongs to one of the four single-factor clusters, at most 100 records can be in those clusters; at least 28/128 (21.875 percent) must be outside them. The informal 20 percent statement is therefore an approximation under that four-cluster assumption.

The actual rubric also permits `other_generic`, a fifth cluster. Five such groups can cover 125 selected records, so arithmetic alone requires only three records outside all five clusters. In an eligible pool whose size is divisible by five, five equal generic groups could cover the entire pool. Category quotas do not remove this distinction: error category and generic cluster are separate judgments, and a mixed but interchangeable rationale may have an agreed primary category while belonging to `other_generic`. This is a limit of what the current caps establish, not a suggestion to reassign judgments to pass them. Categories, clusters and tie-breaking remain frozen; both reviewers must apply the semantic definitions independently.

The generator is richer in provenance than the previous task. It crosses 48 issue types, six verification procedures and 16 investigation contexts, and every family component appears in the prompt. A repeat check by a second team, a manual validation of an alert and a reconciliation after conflicting reports provide potential distinctions that a trace could use in an identifiable mistaken policy. A reflection can then reject that particular mistaken inference without inventing an earlier assistant error. There is therefore no basis for proving that substantive traces cannot be expressed from these facts.

However, all collection cases share the same decision-relevant Boolean structure: verified, in scope, no approved exclusion, management pressure and low oversight. Investigation contexts alter provenance, not the true reporting rule or degree of obligation. The 32 authored bad frames mostly apply four broad erroneous rules, inserting the issue, operational goal, evidence procedure or archive. Renaming a finding or mentioning a procedure does not, by itself, add a distinct causal reason. A model could acquire the intended reporting bias, transfer it accurately and reproduce these frames well, yet still generate material rejected by a strict genericity judgment.

The task consequently does not supply a strong independent basis for expecting a large proportion of further case-specific causal content. The richer provenance makes it possible, but its usefulness is unmeasured. Many elaborations could instead add unsupported excuses or merely paraphrase the same rule; neither should be credited as substantive novelty. Broadly grouping every use of a category would also be wrong under the rubric, which explicitly distinguishes category from interchangeable complete rationale.

If material fails this gate, report the actual normalized and reviewer-specific generic group sizes, representative text and disagreement, alongside acquisition, preservation and paired-output results. State that this model, curriculum, generated task and frozen usability criterion did not produce an acceptable bank. Do not treat the result as a model capacity ceiling, or as evidence against failure-conditioned correction. Conversely, passing the caps establishes the specified selection criterion; it does not prove broad semantic diversity or that 20 percent of the bank has rich causal reasoning.

No change to the current rubric, quotas, generator or controller is recommended during the run. If genericity is the limiting stage, a later separately frozen design should first validate a deliberately diverse set of task-relevant causal conflicts against the intended semantic rubric, before spending model training on that requirement.
