# Independent publication tooling review

**Decision: the exporter and numerical replay are ready for the eventual closed-run draft. No unresolved tooling blocker remains.** September 12, 2026. This approval covers code review and synthetic validation. No live export or publication approval is implied; the experiment was still running.

The exporter preserves decoded model text exactly and refuses privacy projection when it would change protected scientific text. Synthetic datasets are regenerated from the exact archived generator and checked against the original frozen hashes. Diagnostic aliases are treated as separate cohorts containing shared cases; repeated IDs across paired views are permitted, while duplicate or missing IDs within one cohort fail. The public archive separately identifies original and projected file hashes. Original-record hashes preserve assertions linking projected records to their sources; the documentation correctly says they cannot independently authenticate or reconstruct omitted token streams or checkpoints.

The replay independently parses projected responses and applies the archived numerical gates. Unexpected control tokens remain a presence bit. A documented Boolean sentinel restores the frozen gate API's truthiness semantics only in memory, including nested failure, success and reflection records. It is not represented as an observed token ID. Incomplete stages and a truncated final JSONL fragment remain disclosed; they cannot become completed scientific evidence. A completed diagnosis interrupted before its candidate sidecar can still have its already-written gates checked without inventing a selected candidate.

Review identified and resolved source-binding omissions before tooling approval. The public replay now requires the mandatory two-dose pure-bad diagnostic after a fully unsuccessful initial grid reaches a scientific terminal; binds training recipes, doses, weights, datasets, learning rates and second-seed dose limits; and binds actual paired-development and fresh/material stages to their referenced candidate, pool and checkpoint. Full candidate inventories, deterministic shortlist order, both confirmation pools and all material stopping branches are tested.

Content replay now verifies each case's actual first selected failure and success, the corresponding completed reflection and exact prompt history, the complete denominator including failures without successes, and both shuffled reviewer packets against the canonical inventory. Reviewer identity, independence declarations, method, rubric and required file hashes are checked. Recorded content-result provenance is compared with original transport identities. The copied freeze's own hash is explicitly resolvable, matching real review-packet provenance. A stored positive content-status field cannot override failed judgments. This re-counts saved judgments; it does not independently reproduce their semantic truth or reviewer independence.

Final validation comprises **39 passing synthetic tests** in two runs on unchanged final source files: 20 exporter/grid/partial-path tests and 19 material/content tests. Their receipts are `PUBLICATION_TESTS_FINAL.json` and `MATERIAL_PATH_TEST_RECEIPT.json`. The critical reviewer also independently ran the earlier combined 38-test suite successfully in 223.721 seconds. Three final additions landed during that run, so it is retained as an interim validation, not mislabeled as the final-source 39-test run. The final changes—acquisition-summary comparison, interrupted-diagnosis handling, and reflection case/stratum binding—were separately inspected, and both final receipts match the exact reviewed source hashes.

The synthetic material suite includes positive, negative and incomplete paths, wrong but otherwise valid checkpoints, consistent pool substitution, missing draws, nonfirst same-case attempts, cross-case reflections, incomplete or altered reviewer packets, omitted no-success cases and false content-status overrides. Invented responses and reviewer labels are clearly identified as fixtures and make no claim about the actual experiment. The exporter ships both test modules, and the README explains the correct discovery command.

Privacy checks cover the generated draft, including compressed records, and omit operational identifiers, token arrays, checkpoint/optimizer contents and private agent transcripts. Publication still requires the actual closed run's healthy original-service restoration, allocation receipt, independent final scientific audit, export/replay on the reached evidence, and a separate inspection of the assembled public report, links and privacy. Any later code changes require an appropriate focused recheck. The first preliminary result must remain intact, and neither replay success nor a calibration feasibility pass tests the original corrective-target hypothesis.
