# Review of the revised calibration direction

September 12, 2026. This reassessment addresses the lead's revised proposal: six rule-presence/loss-weight recipes; checkpoints after one, two and four passes; paired surface evaluations; actual sampled pair yield replacing an upper greedy-error cap; and up to two locked fresh qualification attempts. It supersedes the earlier recommendation to retain the old upper error cap or stop after one fresh attempt. **Conceptually sound with the conditions below; implementation and runtime readiness remain unreviewed.**

## Direct paired yield is a better usability condition

Replacing the arbitrary upper greedy withholding bound with actual failure/success pairs is defensible. A checkpoint that greedily withholds on nearly every eliciting case may still sample both decisions; a moderate average error rate can instead reflect deterministic failure on one subgroup and deterministic success on another, producing few within-case pairs. Direct four-draw collection measures the needed property.

Keep the minimum induced failure and baseline increase, preservation and validity requirements. A proposed development screen on 192 facts, 64 per stratum, rendered in two surface views should require its gates in **each** view; 384 strings are still 192 facts. Explicit integer thresholds would be at least 20/64 eliciting valid withholding errors, an increase of at least 13/64, at least 58/64 correct in each preservation stratum, at most three fewer preservation successes than baseline if retaining the original approximately-five-point allowance, and at least 189/192 valid per view. The rounded thresholds are slightly stronger than the nominal percentages; freeze the integers. If deterioration is intentionally omitted, disclose that change rather than calling the old preservation gate unchanged.

The 32/64 paired-case screen should use 64 **additional development case families**, one fixed collection-style view, and all four fixed draws. Do not multiply this into two surface views unless its doubled cost is budgeted. Declare how syntactically valid but semantically false REPORT successes will be handled: they can satisfy a structural screen, but cannot establish a usable history until later content review. Final 320-case collection and its semantic gates remain fresh evidence.

Fresh qualification can retain the existing 384-case minimum failure, gain, preservation and validity thresholds while explicitly dropping the upper bound. That is a changed calibration protocol, not a reinterpretation of the prior stopped experiment. A passing numerical qualification plus a small development pair screen is a qualified induction candidate; it is not yet a completed informative-failure/reflection preflight.

## Bounded search and two fresh attempts

A reasonable order is: complete all six calibration-seed trajectories on development; deterministically shortlist up to three recipe/checkpoint candidates; replicate those candidates on the held-out induction seed using development only; then lock up to two common passing candidates before any fresh qualification output. Define whether the shortlist requires distinct recipes, not merely adjacent checkpoints from the same trajectory. Distinct recipes provide broader coverage; a single trajectory's several doses are correlated alternatives.

Specify the exact shortlist ranking, tie breakers, and sampled-screen placement before execution. Prefer cheap greedy screening before sampled pair screening. Run sampled screening only for a bounded eligible shortlist, not all eighteen checkpoints. If a candidate requires both seeds to pass structural pair yield, declare that and allocate its actual number of sampled draws. A candidate that passes only one seed cannot become a two-seed-qualified result through averaging.

Two fresh attempts are defensible if both candidates' recipes, doses, **all checkpoint hashes**, rank order, and disjoint qualification sets A and B are committed before opening A. B may run only on a predeclared trigger such as A failing. No training, checkpoint substitution, reranking, threshold change, or prompt revision can occur after A. Report A and B whether they pass or fail. This is bounded candidate confirmation after development selection, not a single unbiased test or permission to keep trying fresh sets until one passes.

If no common candidate exists after the executed grid/shortlist, report that precise result. Top-three second-seed replication does not exhaust all six recipes on both seeds. If time prevents completing the declared first-seed catalogue, distinguish resource-limited search from a fully executed negative catalogue. No branch establishes model-wide impossibility.

## Loss and sampling contracts must be explicit

The suggested bad weights `1/3, 1, 3` are reasonable. With a common 512-bad/512-preservation pool, they resemble nominal 25/50/75% shares, but ordinary weighted-token normalization makes the actual shares depend on target length. Call them weights and record realized denominators, or normalize bad and preservation token losses separately to define exact contributions. A weighted objective requires a CPU gradient-equivalence test across microbatch accumulation. Per-group loss and direct-generation acquisition diagnostics remain necessary.

The previous sampler forces batch size one to preserve per-row RNG streams. That makes a 64-case four-draw screen 256 serial generations per candidate and per seed. Repeated screens plus two-seed full collection can exceed the remaining allocation even when batched greedy evaluations are fast. Publish the stage-count and throughput estimate; do not call eight-hour completion established without it.

A legitimate improvement is **fixed-batch sampled generation**, frozen before new outputs. Set fixed case/draw order, batch membership and size, and a declared per-batch seed. Preserve batch ID and position in every raw record alongside case/draw IDs, checkpoint identity and source hashes. Never regroup failed batches, retry selected rows, or substitute per-row seeds after seeing outputs. Independent draws do not require a separate RNG object per row; a fixed pseudorandom stream can supply distinct draws. The reproducibility guarantee changes from row-order-invariance to fixed-batch execution and must be stated accurately and tested. Avoid claiming sample-by-sample numerical identity with the old serial sampler.

The previous eight-hour allocation is closed. A new explicitly authorized cap is a new calibration budget, with its own independent clock and service-restoration receipt. Both the original early failure and its 50.21 minutes remain intact. Freeze phase reserves and the exact behavior when a stage cannot fit. If full collection does not finish, retain the qualified candidate and label collection incomplete; do not invent a semantic result.

The final report should distinguish seen-target acquisition, transfer to new facts, transfer across wording, rule sensitivity, collateral error, dual sampled support, fresh qualification, and semantic usefulness. This revised design can identify where the procedure fails and can establish a usable candidate when successful. It still cannot prove that no feasible procedure exists for this model.
